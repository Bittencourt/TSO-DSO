## Blockers

### B1. False `:ok` certificate for V2G EVs on the price-taker and flex-market paths (DEV-07 × SETL-07). Confirmed.

The flag that decides whether the battery certificate runs is set in two places:
`src/settlement/price_taker.jl:125-128` and `src/coordination/flex_clear.jl:442-445`.
- **The flag** uses the shape predicate `p_ch ∧ p_dch ∧ ¬q`. A V2G `ElectricVehicle` has exactly that
  shape, so the flag turns on.
- **The check itself**, `assert_battery_complementarity!` (`src/models/welfare_solve.jl:425-450`), selects devices
  by the `storage_kind` trait. It deliberately skips EVs.
- **The result:** the record says `battery_certificate = :ok`, but no complementarity check ran on the EV.
  `assert_ev_complementarity!` is never called on either path, and `clear_flex_market` has no EV guard. No test
  covers V2G on these paths.

This breaks the honesty policy: a certificate reports a check that never ran. The fix is small. Either route
`has_battery` through the trait and add the EV certificate, or refuse `ElectricVehicle` up front.

### B2. Actors are not wired into the flexibility market or the tariff comparison (ACTOR-01..03 × SETL-07/08). Scope question.

ROADMAP sequenced SETL-07/08 after Phase 44 because they "need the aggregator actor". Phase 45 reused only the
mirror-pair ledger pattern:
- `compare_tariffs` has no `actors` keyword and refuses shared buses;
- `clear_flex_market` builds its ledger without actor wedges.

The literal requirement text is met, because the ledger's "actors" are the DSO, the aggregators and the
wholesale side. Two ways to close this:
- a recorded scope decision, which downgrades B2 to a documented limitation; or
- a guard plus test that rejects actor runs loudly;

or a real integration.
