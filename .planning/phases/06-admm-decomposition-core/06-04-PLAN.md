<objective>
Assemble the hand-rolled ADMM dual-ascent loop `solve_admm` over the Wave-2 subproblems: build
`AGR-OPT[j]` and `DSO-OPT` ONCE, alternate their coefficient-update solves, take the dual step
`λ_j ← λ_j + ρ·R_{p,j}`, stop on the primal residual with a fail-loud maxiter cap, recompute
welfare from primals, gate the converged DSO-OPT with PF-04, and return the price/welfare/
residual tuple. Then turn the Wave-1 RED gate GREEN: the automated cross-validation asserting
ADMM welfare AND duals match the centralized Phase-4 optimum on the 2-bus (dual-sign anchor)
and IEEE-13 ground fixtures — the load-bearing correctness gate (ADMM-04).

Purpose: this is the whole point of the phase. The transactive prices are duals, so ADMM is
correct only if it recovers the centralized optimum AND its DADPs (RESEARCH Pattern 5). The
build-once assertion (ADMM-03) and the fail-loud cap guard the two silent-wrong modes (rebuild
drift, false convergence).

Honest reporting (ADMM-03): Clarabel is `copy_to`-only, so `set_start_value` warm starts are a
NO-OP and the per-iteration re-solve still re-copies to the solver. The ADMM-03 deliverable is
therefore eliminating the JuMP-side model REBUILD (macro expansion + MOI cache population) via
`set_objective_coefficient`, NOT solver warm-start speedups. The build-once `@testitem` asserts
exactly that: num_variables/num_constraints stay fixed and no `Model(`/`build_*` runs in the loop.

Output: `src/admm/solve_admm.jl` (`solve_admm`), `test/test_admm.jl` (GREEN).
</objective>
