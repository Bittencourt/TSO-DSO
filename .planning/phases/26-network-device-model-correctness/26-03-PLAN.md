<objective>
Close the FIX-04 free-energy defect: `PVBattery` and `FourQuadBESS` currently only recurse
`soc[1:T-1] -> soc[t+1]`, so `p_ch[T]`/`p_dch[T]` never appear in any SOC constraint (hour-T
charge/discharge is free). Extend `soc` to `1:(T+1)`, close the recursion over the WHOLE horizon
`t=1:T`, add an optional `soc_terminal` keyword (`nothing` | value | `:cyclic`), and update
`mpc_window.jl`'s terminal-condition wiring (`soc[H]` -> `soc[H+1]`) and its now-obsolete `H==1`
double-pin guard.

Purpose: a battery with `soc0=Emin` and a discharge incentive at hour T must not manufacture
energy from nothing at the horizon boundary.
Output: `PVBattery.jl`/`FourQuadBESS.jl` with the full-horizon SOC recursion; `mpc_window.jl`'s
terminal wiring retargeted; a zero-discharge-at-T regression; an `H=1, terminal_soc=true`
regression that now solves instead of throwing.
</objective>
