finding, not a numerical artifact) — `test_mesh_flow.jl:4` and both `test_mesh_angle_certificate.jl`
items error.

breaks exactness on the uniform diamond' as a finding."

recovery from reactive-power effects) while HONESTLY documenting the newly-discovered exactness
finding rather than silently absorbing it.
