<objective>
Prove the Phase-10 retry/checkpoint machinery and the Phase-11 Benders loop's persistent cut
store hold up at the realistic (~50-100) iteration counts Phase 13's Nash diagonalization loop
will actually drive them to — on a cheap toy fixture, never the full IEEE-13 SOCP oracle — and
close the STATE.md carried blocker that has been assuming, not measuring, the Clarabel
`NUMERICAL_ERROR` rate on planning-layer fixtures.

Purpose: Phase 13 nests a SECOND outer loop (Gauss-Seidel diagonalization) on top of
`solve_stackelberg!`, multiplying the number of Benders solves per distributor per sweep. If the
retry ladder, the checkpoint file naming contract, or the cut store have a latent failure mode at
scale, it is far cheaper to find it here, at single-distributor scale, than after Phase 13 adds a
second nested loop on top.

Output: `test/test_planning_hardening.jl` extended with a load-test `@testitem` (>= 50 Benders
iterations, retry/checkpoint machinery fully active, empirical retry-rate measurement,
checkpoint-round-trip cross-check against the in-memory trace, cut-store growth assertion); the
STATE.md carried Phase-10 blocker updated with the actual Phase-12 measurement.
</objective>
