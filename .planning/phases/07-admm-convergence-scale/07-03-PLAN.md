## Phase Goal

**As a** PhD researcher, **I want to** run ADMM with correct primal+dual stopping,
per-unit-normalized adaptive ρ, and plottable convergence diagnostics that scale to the
IEEE 123-node voltage case, **so that** the decomposed transactive prices are trustworthy
on the research-target regimes.

<objective>
Give the two ADMM subproblem builders the two capabilities the adaptive-ρ loop (07-04) and the
IEEE-123 scale case (07-05) require: (1) a `set_rho!` updater that mutates the QUADRATIC penalty
coefficient in place when ρ changes (the ROADMAP-flagged problem, RESOLVED by the verified
3-arg `set_objective_coefficient`), and (2) a relaxed transit-node guard so a feeder with
zero-injection junction buses (IEEE-123 has ~37) is admissible.

Purpose: Phase 6 built the `(ρ/2)‖·‖²` penalty ONCE and never mutated it, and rejected any
non-root bus lacking an aggregator. Adaptive ρ and IEEE-123 both break those assumptions.
RESEARCH Pattern 1 + Pitfall 5 resolve them without a rebuild and without weakening any gate.

Output: `set_rho!(agr, ρ)` / `set_rho!(dso, ρ)`; a `build_dso_opt` that closes true transit
buses; green set_rho!/build-once/transit @testitems.
</objective>
