<objective>
Close the real correctness gap FIX-09 targets: `fit_baseline`'s (src/pricing/fit.jl) FIT AC-PF
step (SITE 2 of 3) solves a genuine SOC relaxation on a voltage-relaxed feeder but is gated ONLY
by `assert_solved!(model; dual=false)` — `assert_socp_exact!` is NEVER called there, so its
exactness is silently assumed, never verified. Wire the exactness gate in with a caller-
selectable `on_inexact::Symbol = :error` (throw, default) / `:report` (return the certificate)

`ALMOST_OPTIMAL` flake at `tol_gap=1e-10` (Phase-18 finding, genuinely unresolved by every prior
session) — the untried `max_iter` hypothesis is this phase's first concrete experiment; if
solver-intrinsic, bound it behind a measured, named gap tolerance rather than leaving it an
unexplained intermittent failure.

Purpose: `fit_baseline`'s FIT AC-PF is the baseline half of the project's headline +25%
DADP-vs-FIT welfare ratio — an uncertified SOC relaxation there means that headline number could
rest on a fictitious over-current with no warning. FIX-09 makes it "certify exactness, never
skip," matching every other SOCP solve in the codebase.
Output: `fit_baseline`'s FIT AC-PF step exactness-certified; a working `on_inexact` kwarg; the
`ALMOST_OPTIMAL` flake either fixed or explicitly, measurably bounded; a synthetic regression.
</objective>
