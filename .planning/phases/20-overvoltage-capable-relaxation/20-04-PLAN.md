<objective>
Implement OVR-03's documented nonconvex-AC-dual fallback: a pricer that re-solves the EXACT-04
fixture via the already-existing `ACPowerFlow` oracle (zero new solve machinery), tags its result
with a structural `price_status = :local_ac_dual` field plus a local-optimum / not-market-clearing
caveat, and backs it with multi-start agreement evidence (D-09/D-10/D-11).

Purpose: the fallback is what keeps OVR-03's promise ("report-don't-throw... when even the
restricted SOCP cannot certify") honest even in a case where the restriction's own certificate
(plan 20-03) fails. It must never trigger silently or pre-emptively — the CALLER decides, after
observing a `report = true` certificate failure, exactly like every other certificate-driven
decision in this codebase.

Output: `src/models/ac_dual_fallback.jl` (new), one new `TSODSO.jl` include,
`test/test_restricted_branch_flow.jl` gains the CI-gated 2-seed test, and a new quarantined spike
`.planning/spikes/004-ovr-fallback-multistart/` holds the fuller 3-5-seed sweep (Phase 19 D-13
quarantine precedent).
</objective>
