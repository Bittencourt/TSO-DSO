<objective>
Implement PF-04 — the project's headline correctness gate. Add
`assert_socp_exact!(ctx; τ)` that certifies the SOC relaxation is exact per branch
(`max|l·v − (P²+Q²)| < τ`) and THROWS to refuse prices otherwise, and hook it into
`solve_welfare` between `assert_solved!` and the dual read, gated on the presence of the
squared-current variable `:l` (data-driven, no formulation branch). Route the SOCP solver
by trait so a ConvexBranchFlow solve uses the tight-gap SOCP factory.

Purpose: A non-exact relaxation yields physically meaningless duals with no solver error
(RESEARCH Pitfall 1) — prices MUST be refused. This is the "if all else fails, this must
work" gate.
Output: `src/models/exactness.jl`; the exactness hook + trait routing in `solve_welfare`;
the "exact" @testitems (pass on exact, THROW on inexact, hold on high-PV) turn green.
</objective>
