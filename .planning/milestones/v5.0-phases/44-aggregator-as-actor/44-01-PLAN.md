Sign convention (44-RESEARCH Pattern 1): pag is net INJECTION (load negative); AGR coefficient on pag is
-lambda_int - rho*c where lambda_int = -DADP. The wedge adds +m_t to that coefficient. Aggregator profit
is -sum_t m_t*pag_t (= sum m*load, positive on net load). Same m applies to exports (assumption A6; document it).
