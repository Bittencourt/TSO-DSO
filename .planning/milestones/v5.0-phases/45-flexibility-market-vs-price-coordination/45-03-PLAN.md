<objective>
Build the physics of the market. The pipeline is:
1. Stage 1: the baseline at the given tariff.
2. Bids, from the previous plan.
3. Stage 2: the DSO redispatch over bid pseudo-devices, with clearing prices and congested hours.
4. Delivery: tracking re-solves.

`_flex_physics` returns one record per clear key: a (baseline price, n_blocks) pair.

Purpose: "dispatch is identical between rules; only money differs" is easiest to guarantee if the physics is one computed
record that the payment rules (next plan) consume. Every failure mode becomes a status value here.
Output: src/coordination/flex_clear.jl plus tests.
</objective>
