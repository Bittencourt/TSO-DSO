`seeds::NamedTuple` mechanism varies `z0` ONLY. On this game's cost structure, `run_nash!`'s
own default `x_inv0[j] = maximum(z0[j,:])/corridor_cap` has ZERO slack by construction for
ANY `z0`, so every probe combination converges to the BIT-IDENTICAL point regardless of seed
or order — the continuum is real but structurally unreachable through `z0` alone. The fix is
additive and backward-compatible: a `seeds` entry may be EITHER a bare `z0` matrix (unchanged
behavior) OR a `(; z0, x_inv0)` NamedTuple, dispatched by `seed_z0 isa NamedTuple` inside the
existing loop, forwarding `x_inv0` to `run_nash!`'s own pre-existing `x_inv0` keyword
(`run_nash!` itself needs NO changes for this part).

Purpose: make GNE multiplicity honestly visible (never silently reporting one equilibrium as
canonical) and provide a principled selection (the VE) among the continuum.
Output: `src/planning/nash.jl` with the extended `run_nash_probe` and the new
`solve_variational_equilibrium`; `test/test_planning_nash.jl` with the interior-cap fixture,
