orchestrator intervened mid-execution, killed three overlapping stray runs, and launched the

ORCHESTRATOR SUITE CERTIFICATION" marker. The orchestrator (the top-level session that invoked

either (a) appends the certified tallies to `30-FINDINGS.md` directly, or (b) resumes this
plan/a continuation with the tallies so a final write-up step can record them. No task in this
plan's `<action>` or `<verify>` may contain a `Pkg.test` invocation.
