## Integration Findings

Verified by direct source reading and a live `using TSODSO` load test, not from summary narrative:

- **All exports load and are exercised** by at least one live call path: `FourQuadBESS`, `MeshedFlow`,
  `MeshedFeeder`, `RestrictedBranchFlow`, `assert_restriction_exact!`, `certify_angle_recoverable!`,
  `assert_4q_complementarity!`, `build_master_integer`, `run_mpc`, `run_stochastic`.
- **No certificate laundering.** Every new gate carries a distinct, independently-derived tolerance:
  `assert_socp_exact!` (rtol 1e-4 / atol 1e-6), `assert_4q_complementarity!` (rtol 1e-4 / atol 1e-8),
  `assert_restriction_exact!` (cone_rtol 5e-4), `certify_angle_recoverable!` (atol/rtol 0.01),
  Phase 25's freshly-measured per-fixture noise floors. This was the milestone's dominant flagged risk;
  it did not materialize.
- **Solver factory coherent.** Phase 24's `select_optimizer(::MILP)` (`mip_rel_gap => 0.0`,
  `mip_feasibility_tolerance => 1e-9`) and Phase 22's `select_optimizer(::SOCP; attrs...)` keyword
  extension are separate dispatch methods on distinct `ProblemClass` singletons — no conflict, and no
  solver named outside the factory (INFRA-02 holds).
- **`Scenario.jl` schema pressure resolved.** STATE.md flagged Phases 21 and 22 as having identical
  blast radius on this schema-fragile, golden-hash-bearing file. Both landed as additive fields on one
  flat `@kwdef` schema with independent validation and non-overlapping consumers
  (`grep mpc_ src/experiments/run.jl` = 0 matches). The flagged risk was successfully mitigated.
- **All 6 rung literate pages wired** into both the `Literate.markdown` loop and the `pages=` tree in
  `docs/make.jl`. No orphans. (Phase 19 has no page by design — its doc payload folds into Phase 23's
  combined page.)
- **Correction to a milestone scoping assumption:** Phases 21 and 22 do NOT touch `src/admm/` at all —
  both build separate solve paths rather than routing through ADMM. The "shared `src/admm/`
  touch-point" risk named at roadmap time never materialized because those phases never shared the file.

## Assessment

The milestone delivered its seven research-extension axes. The two open gaps are asymmetric and should
be treated differently: **SCALE-04 is controllable** (install an open-source weakdep and re-run some
small rows) while **SCALE-05 is an external memory wall** whose honest non-measurement may legitimately
be the final answer — that is a resource/scope trade-off only the researcher can make, and Phase 25's
verifier correctly escalated it rather than deciding it.

Phase 24's late-arriving lesson is worth carrying forward: its per-cut-validity certificate (a
mechanism test, not an outcome test) caught an invalid Laporte–Louveaux cut that an exhaustive 256-pair
algebra proof had passed cleanly, because that proof validated the algebra against a fixed input and so
could not see a defect in what supplied the input. Three stacked defects sat underneath, plus two more
found by code review. Testing mechanisms rather than outcomes is the transferable practice.
