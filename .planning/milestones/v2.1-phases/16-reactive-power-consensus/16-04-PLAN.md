<objective>
Run the phase's two REQUIRED empirical measurements (16-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 5 / Open
Questions 1-2), which are deliverables (citable findings), not pass/fail tests: (a) the Clarabel
`NUMERICAL_ERROR` flake rate under `reactive_consensus=true` vs `=false` on IEEE-13 AND IEEE-123
(N>=20 repeats each), and (b) a documented resolution of whether a separate `ρ_q` is needed for
reactive residual balancing.

Purpose: STATE.md explicitly requires this phase to "re-measure the flake's rate under
Q-consensus rather than assuming v1.0's rate transfers" — adding `qag_dso` as a genuine decision
variable changes the SOC cone's conditioning (more free variables sharing the same cone at each
load node), and the magnitude of that effect is unknown until measured (16-RESEARCH.md
Assumption A3). The `ρ` vs `ρ_q` question (Open Question 1) is answered directly by Plan
16-02's actual implementation choice: `qag_dso` is pinned via a hard equality
(`qag_dso[j,t] == q_draw[j][t]`) with NO quadratic ρ-penalty term of its own (Assumption A1 — no
live dual-ascent target ever moves), so there is no ρ-penalty weight to tune at all; this is
recorded as the finding rather than re-derived from scratch.
Output: `scripts/reactive_flake_rate.jl` (NEW) + its committed `results/reactive_flake_rate/`
artifact, both citable in the phase's completion notes.
</objective>
