<objective>
Deliver PRICE-01 and PRICE-02 in `src/pricing/dlmp.jl`: extract the day-ahead dynamic price
(DADP/DLMP) as the dual of the nodal active-power balance per node per hour, and decompose it
into energy + loss + congestion + voltage components that provably SUM to the nodal price.

This is the intellectually load-bearing plan of the phase. The thesis gives the decomposition
only qualitatively (Fig 4.5/4.6 narrative), so the four-way split is DERIVED from the KKT
stationarity of the branch-flow variables and the safety net is a HARD assertion that the four
components reconstruct `dual(balance_p[j,t])` within a relative tolerance.

Purpose: Trustworthy, interpretable distribution prices — the product of the whole framework.
Output: `extract_dlmp`, `decompose_dlmp`, and the tests that pin the DADP sign (2-bus) and the
sum-to-nodal-price identity (IEEE-13 + high-PV).
</objective>
